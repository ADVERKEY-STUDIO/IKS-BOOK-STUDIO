# Phase G — Three-spread pilot brief

Status: Three review studies prepared; user direction approval and native PDF verification pending. Phase G is not complete.

## Confirmed constraints

- Focus: Vedic and devotional books, with an individual visual identity for each edition.
- Hanuman Chalisa was an example, not a confirmed pilot selection.
- Paid image generation remains disabled. Use approved imported artwork; agree on an artwork source before producing illustrated proofs.
- Preserve original passages separately from translation and commentary. Do not mark editorial or artistic decisions approved on the user's behalf.

## Selected direction

The user delegated creative and source selection. Selected work: three nonconsecutive Bhagavad Gita verses (2.47, 6.19, 11.12), following IIT Kanpur Gita Supersite. Proposed audience: adult/family readers. Direction: a heritage reading edition, with public-domain Met artwork and original draft English paraphrases. See [source research](../research/phase-g-gita-sources.md) and [review report](PHASE-G-REVIEW-READY.md).

## Original input checklist

1. Selected work and source edition, including the exact passage locations.
2. Intended reader and text layers: original, transliteration, translation, commentary.
3. Visual direction and usable artwork: user-owned imports or appropriately licensed material relevant to the selected work.

Book/source selection is now delegated and resolved for this pilot; final editorial and visual acceptance remains with the user.

## Three compositions

| Sample | Purpose | Design to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet verse spread | Let the original passage lead | Restrained ornament or illustration; generous text space; readable original and supplementary text |
| Detail spread | Give attention to a meaningful subject | Close character, object, symbol, or ornament; deliberate crop; continuity with the book's visual guide |
| Expansive or commentary spread | Test a different reading rhythm | Wide scene if appropriate, otherwise denser commentary; clear hierarchy and controlled page-turn pacing |

These are composition roles, not fixed templates. Scene choices, colors, typography, and density must follow the selected work and reader.

## Execution sequence

1. Inspect the selected source and propose three exact passage allocations. Record source locations and attribution; obtain passage approval.
2. Establish the pilot art guide and any character or symbol references. Confirm appropriateness for this edition.
3. Plan the three spreads and reserve editable text regions before choosing or commissioning artwork.
4. Import finished assets through Production with creator credit and provenance. Record requests and versions; explicitly select approved artwork.
5. Compose all three in Designer using shared physical dimensions and intentional variation. Keep original text source-linked.
6. Export individual physical proofs and inspect dimensions, fonts, image placement, actual-size readability, cropping, gutter, bleed, and effective resolution.
7. Present all three together for source accuracy, cultural appropriateness, continuity, typography, and pacing review. Record changes and repeat affected proof checks.
8. Once the user approves the pilot direction, document completion and push the phase changes to GitHub. Ask before Phase H.
