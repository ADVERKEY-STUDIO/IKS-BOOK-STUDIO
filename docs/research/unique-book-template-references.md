# Distinctive book template references

Researched 14 September 2026. These references address the repeated composition in the current gallery: a new template should change page structure and reading rhythm, not just its palette or border. Adaptation ideas below are design judgments, not seller claims.

## Actual editable template products

| Reference | Verified format | Structural value / proposed IKS use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Maquette — Corentin Billot / Studio Standard](https://studiostandard.com/products/maquette) | 38-page Adobe InDesign system, 1920×1080 px for screen and PDF | Seller specifies project introductions, full-bleed spreads, process documentation, detail crops and typographic specimens. Strongest modular reference: translate these into chapter openings, full artwork, annotated details and Sanskrit type studies. Digital landscape format would need adaptation for a printed book. |
| [Coffee Table Book — StockInDesign](https://stockindesign.com/downloads/coffee-table-book-template/) | 40 pages; INDD, IDML and AI; 9×11 or 11×11 inches with bleeds. Evaluate its live interior preview for a temple/art monograph with changing image density; square format offers a distinct canvas. Photographs and mockups are not included. |
| [Museum Brochure Vol.3 — Bewalrus Studio](https://creativemarket.com/Bewalrusss/12717901-Museum-Brochure-Vol.3) | 20 custom A4 pages; editable InDesign, IDML and PDF | Museum/exhibition editorial candidate for object entries, provenance, caption groups and essays. These IKS uses are proposed adaptations; the seller confirms the page system but does not describe those content roles. Preview images are excluded from the download. |
| [Recipe Book — InDesignSkills](https://indesignskills.com/templates/recipe-book/) | INDD and IDML; 7 spreads plus cover/contents; 7.9×10.9 inches. Publisher offers a free download. | A practical foundation for knowledge organised into ingredients/materials, method and explanation: food traditions, craft processes or ritual practice. The source specifies versatile spreads and type styles, providing a multi-page starting point. |

All four are template product/download pages, not Pinterest boards or book mockups. No purchase or download was made and no direct import into the app was verified. Product descriptions and listed file formats were checked; no claim is made that their licenses permit redistribution inside a template-building application.

## Published books: inspiration only

These are finished books and design case studies, not downloadable templates. Publisher references were researched by the parent agent.

- [Waterlife — Tara Books](https://tarabooks.com/shop/waterlife/): tall 22×36 cm format with Mithila artist Rambharos Jha's aquatic imagery. Direction: a tall artist journal, letting the illustration shape the page rather than occupy a standard side panel.
- [I Saw a Peacock with a Fiery Tail — redesign process](https://tarabooks.com/blog/learning-to-redesign-i-saw-a-peacock-with-a-fiery-tail/): narrative windows and organic die-cut forms, with alternating black and white. Direction: irregular artwork reveals and rhythmic scene changes; a digital interpretation would simulate windows rather than require physical die cuts.
- [Travels through South Indian Kitchens — Tara Books](https://tarabooks.com/shop/travels-through-south-indian-kitchens/): floor plans, sketches, photographs, impressions, recipes and conversations. Direction: field notebook spreads that combine an annotated drawing, short observations, dialogue and process text.

## Recommended next design pass

Start with three different reading systems: Maquette-inspired artwork/detail studies; Waterlife-inspired tall artist pages; and Kitchens-inspired annotated field notes. Give each a cover, chapter opener, dense reading spread and image-led spread. Compare those at the same size with identical content before adding them to the app, so structural variety is visible without relying on different images or colors.
