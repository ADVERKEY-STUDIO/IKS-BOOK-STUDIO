# Real published book candidates for the inspiration gallery

Research date: 11 September 2026. Scope: modern devotional, epic, and Indian illustrated books. This is a discovery register, not a claim that every book is visually excellent. Publisher descriptions establish identity and potential relevance; they do not substitute for inspecting interiors. The lead research task records visual assessments separately.

No application code changed. No previews have been licensed, downloaded for gallery publication, or approved for commercial reuse. All reuse permissions below remain **unverified**. Publisher-hosted previews are links for inspection, not proof of a republication license. Traditional-art books are adjacent design references, not Vedic scriptures; modern retellings are not source-text editions.
